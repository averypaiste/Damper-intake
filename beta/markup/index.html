Damper Markup

Put damper labels on a floor plan: a label with the ID, a line, and a small square on the spot.

  1. Open the floor plan PDF (or a screenshot).
  2. Zoom in, type the damper ID, and tap its spot.
  3. Drag the label where you want it, pick the color, then Export.
