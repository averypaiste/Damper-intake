Troubleshooting

How this works

Red dot

Someone started this damper, but some info is still missing or doesn't match. Tap the damper. The top of the page tells you what to fix.

Tap a damper

Opens its page. Fill in what you need and add photos. Everything is saved when you tap the × or go to the next damper, and sent by itself when there is signal.

Copy and paste in the table

Hold a cell (for example Inspection Note) until the black bar shows at the bottom. Then tap other cells in the same column to paste it into them. Tap Done when finished.

Move a photo to another box

In the table, hold a photo until it lifts up, then drag it onto the right photo box in the same row (or let go and tap the box). Tick Keep a copy to copy it instead of moving it.

‹ Jobs

Shows every job you can open. Search by job number or hospital, tap ☆ to pin a job to the top, and tap a job to open it.

⬇ Offline

Saves one job on your phone so it works with no signal: Today only clears itself tomorrow, Keep until I remove it stays. Only the fields in the job's Tech View and small copies of its photos are saved. Jobs that are not saved still work whenever you have signal.

🗺 Map

Shows the job's map from its Drawings table. It is saved on your device by itself, so it opens with no signal. Tap Open to look at it, or Save to Files to keep a copy (on iPhone, pick Save to Files in the share menu).

↻ Refresh lists

Gets the newest dampers, info and photos. Tap it when you have Wi-Fi or signal, and before you go somewhere with no signal.

No signal? No problem.

Keep working. Your entries are saved on your phone and sent by themselves when the signal comes back. waiting to sync means it hasn't been sent yet.

Unfinished work is kept

If the app closes while you are on a damper page, what you typed and photographed is kept. The damper shows draft and it is sent the next time you open it and close it again.

Photos

Photos already taken show in each box. Tap Take photo to use the camera, Camera roll to pick from your photos, or Choose file for anything else. Photos from the camera are also kept in your phone's Photos app.

Menus (Pass, Fail...)

Tap a title to open or close that group.

Date order

The Date box above search puts the list in order by inspection date (newest or oldest first). Set it back to any order to see the Pass / Fail groups again.

Messages

Normally empty. If it has an orange dot, something went wrong that needs fixing. Open it and tell Avery what it says, including the code number (like 422). Tap Copy for Avery to copy it so you can paste it into a text.

The top bar color

Green means you have signal. Red means you don't. Both are fine. Keep working either way.
