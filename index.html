Troubleshooting

How this works

Red dot

Someone started this damper, but some info is still missing or doesn't match. Tap the damper. The top of the page tells you what to fix.

Tap a damper

Opens its page. Fill in what you need, add photos, then tap Save entry. Tap the × to close the page.

↻ Refresh lists

Gets the newest dampers, info and photos. Tap it when you have Wi-Fi or signal, and before you go somewhere with no signal.

No signal? No problem.

Keep working. Your entries are saved on your phone and sent by themselves when the signal comes back. waiting to sync means it hasn't been sent yet.

Unfinished work is kept

Anything you type or photograph is kept even if you close the page without saving. The damper shows draft, but it is not sent until you tap Save entry.

Photos

Photos already taken show in each box. Tap Take photo to use the camera, or Upload file to pick one you already have. Photos from the camera are also kept in your phone's Photos app.

Menus (Pass, Fail...)

Tap a title to open or close that group.

Sort

At the top. Puts the list in order by inspection date. Tap again to switch the direction, then again to turn it off.

Messages

Normally empty. If it has an orange dot, something went wrong that needs fixing. Open it and tell Avery what it says, including the code number (like 422). Tap Copy for Avery to copy it so you can paste it into a text.

The top bar color

Green means you have signal. Red means you don't. Both are fine. Keep working either way.
