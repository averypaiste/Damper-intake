Damper Markup

Put damper labels on a floor plan: a red circle on the spot, an arrow, and a colored box with the ID.

  1. Open the floor plan PDF (or a screenshot).
  2. Zoom in, type the damper ID, and tap its spot.
  3. Drag the box where you want it, pick the color, then Save.
